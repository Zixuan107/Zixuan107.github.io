About

I'm a student at the University of Michigan studying Computer Science and Business through the Integrated Business and Engineering dual-degree program, with an intended minor in mathematics.

Currently, I am an AI researcher at LAUNCH Lab at the University of Michigan, focusing on improving the efficiency of large language models and agentic AI systems. Prior to this, I conducted AI research at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I focused on designing deep learning models to capture statistical patterns in cryptographic algorithms.

I enjoy building personal projects that apply AI to real-world problems, from IrrigOpt AI for precision agriculture to computational models for market analysis and financial decision-making. Outside of research and building projects, I enjoy playing soccer, hiking, exploring new places, and cooking for friends and family. I'm also interested in entrepreneurship and markets, and I like learning about how technology can turn ideas into useful products.

Investigated deep learning approaches to cryptanalysis of reduced-round Data Encryption Standard (DES), studying how neural networks can identify statistical structure within generated cryptographic datasets.

Designed and evaluated deep neural network architectures for key-recovery experiments, achieving up to 96.8% classification accuracy on generated cryptanalytic datasets.

ResNet-Based Neural Cryptanalysis of CRAFT-64

Investigated CNN- and ResNet-based neural distinguishers designed to learn higher-order cryptographic patterns from VDS-generated datasets for the CRAFT-64 block cipher.
